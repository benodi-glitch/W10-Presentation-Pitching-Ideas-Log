Group Presentation Listening Log

Audience Task — Marketing Pitch Presentations

How to use this log

Your group is the audience. Listen actively and record keywords or short phrases. Do not write a full summary.
  1. Product & Audience: What did they sell, and who is it for?
  2. Strategies: What two marketing actions did they propose?
  3. Message & Slogan: What was their key message? What was the slogan?
  4. Persuasion: Which line, device or technique worked best on you?
  5. Would you buy? Yes, maybe or not yet, and one reason.
